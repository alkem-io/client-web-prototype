You are designing video call backgrounds for Alkemio — a European, steward-owned, open-source platform for collaboration between organisations on complex public challenges. The primary positioning line is "Collaboration in the spaces between organisations." These backgrounds will be used by the team on Microsoft Teams calls with Dutch public-sector partners. They need to feel institutional, restrained, and European — not startup-flashy. The logo (SVG) and any tagline will be placed manually afterwards; do not include any text or logos in the artwork.

The five concepts each express a different facet of Alkemio's positioning: the space between organisations, shared ground, multi-stakeholder convergence, open infrastructure, and steward-ownership as institutional trust. Each concept comes in a light and a dark variant using the same design system.

Create a Figma file with 10 separate pages. Each page contains a single 1920 × 1080 px frame. The first 5 pages are light variants, the last 5 are dark variants of the same designs. All artwork is vector shapes and gradients only — no images, no raster, no text, no logos.

Colour tokens used throughout:
- INK: #1D384A
- CYAN: #09BCD4
- CYAN-DEEP: #0787A5
- SLATE: #2D546A
- TINT: #F1F5F9
- WHITE: #FFFFFF
- DARK-BASE: #162D3B

---

PAGE 1: "The Space Between — Light"

Frame: 1920 × 1080 px. Background fill: linear gradient 135° from #FFFFFF (top-left) to #F1F5F9 (bottom-right).

Layer "Left Landmass": A large rounded organic shape (superellipse / squircle, not a perfect circle). Centre at x:280, y:540. Width 720px, height 880px. Corner radius 180px. Rotation -8°. Fill: solid #1D384A at 6% opacity. No stroke. Gaussian blur 40px.

Layer "Right Landmass": A large rounded organic shape. Centre at x:1640, y:540. Width 680px, height 820px. Corner radius 200px. Rotation 5°. Fill: solid #1D384A at 5% opacity. No stroke. Gaussian blur 48px.

Layer "Glow": An ellipse centred at x:960, y:540. Width 600px, height 900px. Fill: radial gradient from #09BCD4 at 3% opacity (centre) to transparent (edge). Gaussian blur 80px. Blend mode Screen.

The centre band (x:580 to x:1340, full height) must remain open and nearly featureless. The bottom-left corner (x:0–480, y:800–1080) must be completely quiet — no shapes, no edges.
