Picking contributors you don't know by name

When a Lead turns on Manual selection for a Contributors post, the picker is a bare search box. Nothing appears until you type, so you can only add people whose names you already remember. Below are three ways to show the space's community up front. All three work: tick people, search, remove chips.

Only people and organizations in this space's community are listed. Production already fixed the subspaces half of the ticket this week, so this page covers contributors only.

Option A

An open list, like the new subspace picker

Everyone in the community is listed straight away with a checkbox. Typing narrows the list. Picked people show as chips above the search box, with a count and Clear selection underneath.

Good at

  • You see who's there without typing anything.
  • It looks and works exactly like the subspace picker, so the two halves of the ticket match.
  • The checkbox shows who's already in, so the list doesn't jump around as you pick.

Watch out

  • It makes the dialog taller, roughly eight rows.
  • With 412 people the list is long. Leads are listed first, but search does most of the work.
Change to production: new picker, same design as the subspace one
Option B

A dropdown that opens when you click the box

Simone's simpler idea. The search box stays as it is, but clicking it opens a dropdown with everyone before you type. Picking someone keeps the dropdown open so you can pick the next one.

Good at

  • The smallest change. Production's picker stays, only "show suggestions on click" is new.
  • Keeps the dialog compact. The list only appears while you're picking.

Watch out

  • The list is hidden until you click, so a Lead may still not realise they can browse.
  • The dropdown covers Default display while it's open.
  • For 412 people it shows the first 50 and asks you to keep typing.
Change to production: small, one new behaviour
Option C

An open list split into tabs, Leads first

Like Option A, but split by People, Organizations and Virtual contributors, with Leads and members in separate groups. A Select all Leads link picks the whole lead team in one click.

Good at

  • Easiest to scan in a big community. Each tab is shorter, and you can see who the Leads are.
  • Common picks, such as "our Leads", take one click.

Watch out

  • More to build and more on screen. The tabs repeat the post's "contributor types" setting.
  • Search only covers the open tab, so someone in another tab can be missed.
Change to production: new picker, more than A
