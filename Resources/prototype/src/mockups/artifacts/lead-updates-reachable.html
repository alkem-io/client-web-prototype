Getting to an update from the lead

Today a notification about a lead's update drops you on the space home page, and the update itself can't be opened on its own. This mockup puts the pinned update at the top of the feed, keeps production's Updates box in the sidebar, and makes the notification open the update it is about. Everything inside the window can be clicked.

  1. Open the bell and click a notification. The third one is about an older update, not the latest.
  2. Click the pinned update or the Updates box in the sidebar.
  3. Switch between option A and option B above the window to compare what opens.
  4. Watch the address bar: each update gets its own link. Use Copy link and the back arrow.
Opens to

Option A

All updates, scrolled to the one you clicked

The ticket's suggestion. Production's existing Updates window opens, scrolls to the update and highlights it for a moment.

  • Reuses what's there. The same window "See all updates" opens today, with one new trick: it can open on a given update.
  • Context for free. Newer and older updates are right there to scroll through.
  • Weaker on a long list. With many updates, the highlight is the only thing saying which one you came for.
Option B

Just that update, with a way to the rest

A window with only the update you clicked. Newer and older steps through the others, and "See all updates" opens the full list.

  • No doubt what you're looking at. One update, full text, nothing else competing.
  • Feels like a page of its own. That fits a link someone sends you.
  • One more thing to build. A second window next to the list, and two ways to read the same updates.

The same in both options

  • The notification opens the update it is about, on the right space or subspace, even when newer updates have been posted since.
  • The pinned update and the sidebar preview can be clicked. Both open the full update, not just the cut-off preview.
  • Every update has its own link, like ?update=… at the end of the space address. Copy link puts it on the clipboard; opening it lands on the same view.
  • Closing the window takes the link back to the plain space address, and back in the browser goes to where you were before.
  • "New" on the pinned card disappears once you've opened that update.
  • To decide later: whether the sidebar Updates box stays once the pinned card exists. Here they show the same latest update twice.
