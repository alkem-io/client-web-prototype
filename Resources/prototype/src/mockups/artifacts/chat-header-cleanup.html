client-web#10378 · chat header

A chat header that stays in the chat

Three things are wrong with the top of a chat window today. This board shows each one as it is now and as it could be, with options where the issue leaves a decision open. Two calls are yours: the icon for the chat's settings, and how the group avatar looks with three or more people.

  1. The gear sends you away. It opens your notification settings page and closes the chat.
  2. The people icon is the real settings, but it looks like a member list.
  3. The group avatar is half empty. It is a 2 × 2 grid, so two people fill only the top row.
Fix · no decision needed

The gear leaves the chat header

Inside a chat, the gear goes. In the chat list it stays, because there it means your chat settings in general, which is what it opens.

  • One-to-one chats end up with only back, the person and close. Production has no settings for a one-to-one chat, so there is nothing to point to.
  • Group chats keep one button for the group's settings. Which icon is the next row.
  • The Guidance chat is not touched. Its info and clear buttons already belong to that chat.

Decision 1 · the icon

Which icon opens the group's settings

The button opens production's existing dialog: rename the group, change its picture, add or remove people, leave. Each option shows the tooltip you get on hover. The button's spoken name is the same text.

Whichever icon wins, tapping the group's picture or name could open the same dialog. That is where most chat apps put it, so people who never notice the icon still find it.

Chosen: D, plus tap the picture or name

Decision 2 · the avatar

A group avatar that fills the circle

The avatar shows the other people in the group, not you. So a group of three is two faces. Production already uses one component for the list and the header; the only difference is size (40 and 32 pixels). Fixing the component fixes both.

  • Two faces: halves. No decision.
  • Three faces: a half and two quarters, or three slices.
  • Four or more: the four quarters, or three faces and a count.

A group with its own picture keeps showing that picture, as today.

Chosen: half + quarters, count from five

Decided on 7 October

The "more" menu (D), because a chat's own options such as mute and pin can join it later without another icon. Tapping the group's picture or name opens its settings too. Avatars use half and quarters, with a count from five. The rest of this section is the original question.

Decision 1

Icon for the group's settings: A, B, C or D?

I would go with C, the info icon, because the Guidance chat already uses it in that spot for the same idea.

Decision 1b

Also open settings by tapping the group's picture or name?

An extra way in, not a replacement. I would add it.

Decision 2

Three faces: half and quarters, or slices?

I would go with half and quarters. Faces stay upright, and it moves to four quarters without changing shape.

Decision 3

Five or more faces: a count, or the first four?

I would show the count. It tells you how big the group is, which four faces do not.
