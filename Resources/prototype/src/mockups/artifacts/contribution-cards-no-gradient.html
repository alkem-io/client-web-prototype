product #2207 · response cards

Response cards without the gradient

Memo, Whiteboard and Document responses still put their title on a blue fade over the preview. It's one of the last gradients left on the platform. The proposal swaps the fade for a solid band in the card colour, with the title and author in normal text, the same way a Post response reads.

The blue wash on hover stays. It now covers only the preview, so the title stays readable while you hover. Hover over any card on this page to try it.
