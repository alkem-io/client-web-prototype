New activity indicators — concepts

Five candidate visual treatments for the “something changed here” signal, applied live across all five surfaces. Switch treatment and colour in the bar above; everything below updates at once. The behaviour is real, not faked: tabs and cards clear when you visit them, posts clear when you scroll them into view.

My read

  • A (solid dot) for everything, at 6px. It is the only option that works identically on rows, tabs, cards and posts, which matters more than any individual cell looking nicer.
  • D is the most beautiful on cards and the most useless on tabs. Mixing D on cards with A on tabs gives you two signals to learn instead of one.
  • E is the one to avoid. Bold text says “unfinished work”; a dot says “news”. The client asked for the second.

3 · Don’t collide with the bell

Two unread systems, two visual languages

The notification bell already exists and means “this concerns you”. Activity dots mean “this place changed”. They must not look like the same thing, or people will assume the dots are also things they’re obliged to act on.

🔔3
Red, counted, cornered. Directed at you. Demands action.
Coloured, numberless, inline. Ambient. Invites, never demands.
  • Never render activity dots in --destructive red, and never give them a visible count.
  • The hidden count still goes to screen readers via the label, so assistive tech gets more, not less.
4 · Control

One switch, in General settings

Toggle this off and every dot on this page disappears. No granularity — per-surface controls are a settings tax nobody pays.

Show new activity indicators
Display a small dot on spaces, tabs, cards and posts that have changed since you last looked.
Mark everything as read
Clears every indicator across all your spaces. Undoable for a few seconds afterwards.
