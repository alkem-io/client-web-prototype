You are designing video call backgrounds for Alkemio — a European, steward-owned, open-source platform for collaboration between organisations on complex public challenges. The positioning line is "Collaboration in the spaces between organisations." These will be used on Microsoft Teams calls with Dutch public-sector partners. They should feel premium, modern, and confident — not generic corporate, not startup-noisy. The logo will be placed manually; do not include text or logos.

These are artistic, visually rich compositions. Use bold gradients, saturated colour, layered depth, and confident shapes. The palette is built from Alkemio's brand:
- INK (deep navy): #1D384A
- CYAN (brand accent): #09BCD4
- CYAN-DEEP: #0787A5
- SLATE: #2D546A
- TINT: #F1F5F9

Key constraints: The vertical centre band (roughly x:580–1340) should stay relatively open (a person sits there), and the bottom-left corner (x:0–400, y:880–1080) should be a calm zone for the logo. Beyond that, be bold.

Create a Figma file with 10 pages. Pages 1–5 are light variants, pages 6–10 are dark variants.

---

PAGE 1: "Aurora — Light"

Frame: 1920 × 1080 px.

Background: solid #F1F5F9.

Layer "Wash Base": A full-frame rectangle. Fill: linear gradient 160° — #FFFFFF at 0%, #E8F4F8 at 50%, #F1F5F9 at 100%.

Layer "Flow 1": A large organic blob shape (like a stretched, melted ellipse — think lava lamp). Positioned at the top-right: centre x:1500, y:200. Width ~900px, height ~500px. Fill: linear gradient 45° from #09BCD4 at 60% opacity to #0787A5 at 40% opacity. Gaussian blur 60px. The shape should have irregular, smooth edges — not a perfect ellipse.

Layer "Flow 2": Another organic blob. Positioned at bottom-left: centre x:350, y:850. Width ~700px, height ~450px. Fill: linear gradient 200° from #2D546A at 50% opacity to #1D384A at 35% opacity. Gaussian blur 50px.

Layer "Flow 3": A thin, elongated organic streak (like a ribbon or aurora band). Runs diagonally from upper-left (x:100, y:300) to lower-right (x:1800, y:700). Width varies from 80px to 200px along its length. Fill: linear gradient along its length from #09BCD4 at 30% opacity to #0787A5 at 15% opacity. Gaussian blur 30px.

Layer "Highlight": A small ellipse at x:1600, y:150. Width 300px, height 200px. Fill: radial gradient from #09BCD4 at 40% to transparent. Gaussian blur 40px. Blend mode Screen.

The composition sweeps colour from top-right toward bottom-left, with the centre staying open and breathable. The flows overlap at the edges creating depth.

PAGE 2: "Terrain — Light"

Frame: 1920 × 1080 px.

Background: linear gradient 180° from #FFFFFF (top) to #E2EDF2 (bottom).

This concept uses layered, curved horizontal bands — like a topographic cross-section or abstract hills — stacked from bottom to top with decreasing opacity, creating depth and a sense of landscape.

Layer "Hill 1" (foreground, bottom): A shape filling the bottom portion of the frame. Top edge is a smooth, gentle wave curve (cubic bezier) starting at (0, 820), peaking at around (500, 780), dipping to (960, 850), rising to (1400, 790), ending at (1920, 830). Bottom edge is the frame bottom. Fill: solid #1D384A at 85% opacity.

Layer "Hill 2": Top edge is a wave curve approximately 100px above Hill 1: (0, 720), (400, 680), (900, 730), (1500, 670), (1920, 710). Extends down to frame bottom. Fill: solid #2D546A at 60% opacity.

Layer "Hill 3": Top edge wave: (0, 600), (350, 560), (800, 610), (1300, 550), (1920, 590). Fill: solid #0787A5 at 35% opacity.

Layer "Hill 4" (most distant): Top edge wave: (0, 480), (500, 450), (1000, 490), (1600, 440), (1920, 470). Fill: solid #09BCD4 at 20% opacity.

The person sits in the open sky area. The layered hills give a sense of shared landscape — "common ground" — with rich colour and depth but all weight at the bottom half.

PAGE 5: "Tide — Light"

Frame: 1920 × 1080 px.

Flowing, organic curves that sweep across the frame — like currents of water or ribbons of wind. Multiple layers create rhythm and movement.

Background: linear gradient 135° from #FFFFFF (top-left) to #EDF5F7 (bottom-right).

Layer "Current 1": A wide flowing ribbon shape. Path starts at (-100, 900), curves through (400, 700), (960, 750), (1500, 600), ends at (2020, 650). Width of the ribbon: ~180px. Fill: linear gradient along path from #1D384A at 70% opacity to #2D546A at 40% opacity. Gaussian blur 8px.

Layer "Current 2": A flowing ribbon. Path: (-100, 700), (300, 550), (800, 600), (1300, 450), (1920, 500). Width: ~140px. Fill: linear gradient along path from #0787A5 at 55% opacity to #09BCD4 at 30% opacity. Gaussian blur 6px.

Layer "Current 3": A thinner ribbon. Path: (0, 500), (500, 400), (1100, 450), (1700, 350), (1920, 370). Width: ~80px. Fill: solid #09BCD4 at 25% opacity. Gaussian blur 12px.

Layer "Current 4": Wide, faint ribbon. Path: (0, 1000), (600, 880), (1200, 920), (1920, 850). Width: ~250px. Fill: solid #1D384A at 20% opacity. Gaussian blur 20px.

Layer "Foam highlight": Small ellipses scattered along Current 1 (3-4 of them, each ~60×40px). Fill: #FFFFFF at 40% opacity. Gaussian blur 15px. These create luminous highlights on the flow.

The currents sweep from left to right with a gentle upward drift, creating a sense of movement and connection. They stay in the lower half and edges, leaving the upper-centre open for the person.
