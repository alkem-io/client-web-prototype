You are designing video call backgrounds for Alkemio — a European, steward-owned, open-source platform for collaboration between organisations on complex public challenges. These will be used on Microsoft Teams calls with Dutch public-sector partners.

The messaging that shapes these concepts:
- "Collaboration in the space between organisations." (primary)
- Spaces to power your collaboration.
- Coordinate. Co-create. Collaborate.
- Shared workspaces for shared challenges.
- European. Steward-owned. Open-source.

Each concept must visually express one of these ideas — not just look nice. Use rich gradients, confident shapes, and the Alkemio palette at full strength. The logo will be placed manually afterwards — no text or logos in the artwork.

Palette:
- INK (deep navy): #1D384A
- CYAN (brand accent): #09BCD4
- CYAN-DEEP: #0787A5
- SLATE: #2D546A
- TINT: #F1F5F9

Constraints: The vertical centre band (x:580–1340) stays breathable for the person. Bottom-left corner (x:0–400, y:880–1080) stays calm for logo placement.

Create a Figma file with 10 pages. Pages 1–5 are light variants, pages 6–10 are dark variants.
